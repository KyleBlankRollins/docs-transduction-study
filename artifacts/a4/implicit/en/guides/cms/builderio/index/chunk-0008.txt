Second, Add a new webhook by clicking on Webhook . Paste the URL generated by your hosting provider into the Url field. Finally, Click on Show Advanced under the URL field and toggle the option to select Disable Payload . With the payload disabled, Builder sends a simpler POST request to your hosting provider, which can be helpful as your site grows. Click Done to save this selection. With this webhook in place, whenever you click the Publish button in the Builder editor, your hosting provider rebuilds your site - and Astro fetches the newly published data for you.
