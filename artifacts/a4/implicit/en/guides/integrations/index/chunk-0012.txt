tead. Categories Section titled “Categories” In addition to the required astro-component , astro-integration , or withastro keyword, special keywords are also used to automatically organize packages. Including any of the keywords below will add your integration to the matching category in our integrations library. The table below is given as a list. For Accessibility: keywords is a11y , accessibility . For Adapters: keywords is astro-adapter . For Analytics: keywords is analytics . For CSS + UI: keywords is css , ui , icon , icons , renderer . For Frameworks: keywords is renderer . For Content Loaders: keywords is astro-loader . For Images + Media: keywords is media , image , images , video , audio . For Performance + SEO: keywords is performance , perf , seo , optimization . For Dev Toolbar: keywords is devtools , dev-overlay , dev-toolbar . For Utilities: keywords is tooling , utils , utility . Packages that don’t include any keyword matching a category will be shown as Uncategorized .
