Running your NightwatchJS tests Section titled “Running your NightwatchJS tests” You can run a single test or several tests at once, testing one or multiple browsers. By default, your test results will be shown in the terminal. Optionally, you can open the HTML Test Reporter to show a full report and filter test results.
